# Privacy Policy - Online Wallpaper

Last updated: 2026-05-21

## Overview
Online Wallpaper lets users browse wallpaper images from the Pexels API, mark favorites locally, share image links/files, and set wallpapers on supported devices.

## Data We Collect
- We do not require account registration.
- We do not collect personal profile data (name, email, phone) inside the app.
- We do not sell user data.

## Data Processed by the App
- Network requests to Pexels API to fetch wallpaper metadata and image URLs.
- Favorite wallpaper IDs stored locally on device (SharedPreferences).
- Temporary image files may be created locally for sharing or wallpaper operations.

## Permissions
- Internet/network access is used to load wallpaper content from the API.
- Wallpaper permission is used on Android to set selected wallpaper.

## Third-Party Services
- Pexels API (content provider)

Users should also review third-party provider policies.

## Data Retention
- Favorites remain on-device until user clears app data or uninstalls the app.
- Temporary files are stored in app temporary storage and can be removed by the system.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under the age required by applicable local law.

## Contact
For policy questions, contact the app publisher via your Play Console/App Store contact channel.
